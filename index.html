Chapter I  —  The Approach

Markets reward patience, not noise.

Trading and investing across markets, and teaching others to do the same.

Most traders only know how to make money when the market's going up. The whole game changes in a bear market, and again when markets go sideways. Learning to operate across all three is what separates a trader who lasts from one who doesn't.

That's what I teach, and why I do it: trading and investing, done properly, give you control over your time, where you live, and how you build a life.
